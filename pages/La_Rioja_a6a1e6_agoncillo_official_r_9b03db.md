## Introduction

The Agoncillo aerodrome sighting of 20 July 1978 is [La Rioja]({{ 'what-really-happened-in-la-riojas-ufo/' | relative_url }})’s most important UFO case for a simple reason: it is the province’s one clearly identifiable entry in Spain’s public Defence UFO archive. It was not a long encounter, a radar chase, or a dramatic landing story. It was a short night-time observation by three military witnesses at the Agoncillo aerodrome near Logroño, recorded in a small official file and declassified years later. That makes it a useful test case for what “unidentified” really means in a provincial UFO history.



<img src="{{ "/assets/images/La_Rioja_a6a1e6_agoncillo_official_r_9b03db-overview.webp" | relative_url }}" alt="Overview image for Agoncillo" loading="eager" decoding="sync" fetchpriority="high">

The best reading is cautious. The file supports that an unusual lighted object was reported from a military airfield, by on-duty personnel, with enough consistency to be written up through Air Force channels. It does not prove an extraordinary craft. The record is brief, the observation lasted only about five minutes, the witnesses disagreed on details, and the surviving public material does not show a firm technical explanation or a full investigation trail. The case still matters because it sits in the narrow space between a weak anecdote and a solved misidentification: officially recorded, locally significant, but still evidentially limited.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.eldiario.es/la-rioja/ovnis-en-la-rioja-defensa-saca-a-la-luz-un-posible-caso-en-agoncillo_1_11094435.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: eldiario.es">[elDiario.es]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">eldiario.es</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ovnis en la rioja defensa saca a la luz un posible caso en agoncillo 1 11094435</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">OVNIS en La Rioja: Defensa saca a la luz un posible caso en Agoncillo...</span></span></span>

## What the military witnesses reported

The reported event took place at about 1.20 am on 20 July 1978 at the Agoncillo aerodrome, then a military aviation site near Logroño. According to later regional reporting based on the declassified file, a soldier on duty at the main gate alerted the guardroom after seeing something unusual in the sky. An ensign and a corporal went to the post and also observed the object for around five minutes.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.eldiario.es/la-rioja/ovnis-en-la-rioja-defensa-saca-a-la-luz-un-posible-caso-en-agoncillo_1_11094435.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: eldiario.es">[elDiario.es]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">eldiario.es</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ovnis en la rioja defensa saca a la luz un posible caso en agoncillo 1 11094435</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">OVNIS en La Rioja: Defensa saca a la luz un posible caso en Agoncillo...</span></span></span>

The common elements in the testimony are the strongest part of the case. The three witnesses reportedly agreed that the object was at roughly 1,000 metres, moved from east to west, travelled slowly, and made no sound. They also agreed that it had a brighter central white light that flashed intermittently. Those points matter because the account was not a single-person memory later shaped by folklore; it was a short observation shared by military personnel at the time and preserved in an official Defence record.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.eldiario.es/la-rioja/ovnis-en-la-rioja-defensa-saca-a-la-luz-un-posible-caso-en-agoncillo_1_11094435.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: eldiario.es">[elDiario.es]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">eldiario.es</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ovnis en la rioja defensa saca a la luz un posible caso en agoncillo 1 11094435</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">OVNIS en La Rioja: Defensa saca a la luz un posible caso en Agoncillo...</span></span></span>

The details were not perfectly consistent. Two witnesses described a diamond-shaped object, while the third described a triangular form. One account referred to four white lights at the vertices of the diamond, while another description was less precise, and the triangular version involved a white light at each point. This does not make the report worthless, but it does weaken any confident reconstruction of the object’s physical shape. At night, a pattern of lights can easily be read as a solid body, especially during a brief sighting with no distance measurement, radar track, or photographic record.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.eldiario.es/la-rioja/ovnis-en-la-rioja-defensa-saca-a-la-luz-un-posible-caso-en-agoncillo_1_11094435.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: eldiario.es">[elDiario.es]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">eldiario.es</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ovnis en la rioja defensa saca a la luz un posible caso en agoncillo 1 11094435</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">OVNIS en La Rioja: Defensa saca a la luz un posible caso en Agoncillo...</span></span></span>

The most quoted line from the case comes from the ensign’s description: the object was not an aircraft, but “a strange thing”. That sentence is vivid, but it should be read carefully. It records the witness’s immediate judgement, not a technical exclusion of every aircraft, balloon, satellite, atmospheric, or optical explanation. The official value of the testimony lies less in that phrase than in the combination of place, time, multiple witnesses, reported direction, lights, silence and military documentation.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.finanzas.com/hemeroteca/no-es-un-avion-lo-que-estamos-viendo-es-una-cosa-extrana_13504249_102.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: finanzas.com">[Finanzas.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">finanzas.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">«No es un avión lo que estamos viendo, es una cosa extraña»</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">«No es un avión lo que estamos viendo, es una cosa extraña»</span></span></span>

## What the declassified file actually shows

The official catalogue entry identifies the case as “Avistamiento de fenómenos extraños en Agoncillo (Logroño): 20 de Julio de 1978”, authored by the Operational Air Command’s staff intelligence section. The record is listed as a three-page manuscript, with the call number 780720, and was declassified under JEMA 684 on 15 February 1995. Its subject and place metadata link it to UFO observations and encounters in Logroño and La Rioja.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://bibliotecavirtual.defensa.gob.es/BVMDefensa/exp_ovni/en/consulta/registro.do?id=38342" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: bibliotecavirtual.defensa.gob.es">[Biblioteca Virtual Defensa]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">bibliotecavirtual.defensa.gob.es</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on gob.es.</span></span></span>

That catalogue evidence is important because it fixes the case in the official Spanish Air Force UFO corpus, rather than leaving it as a newspaper legend or a retold local mystery. The Ministry of Defence’s presentation of the wider collection explains that Spain began declassifying documents on “strange phenomena sightings” in 1991, deposited a physical copy in the Air Force Central Library in 1992, and later made the digitised files available through the Virtual Defence Library. The collection comprises 80 files and around 1,900 pages, covering Spanish airspace cases from 1962 to 1995.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://bibliotecavirtual.defensa.gob.es/BVMDefensa/exp_ovni/es/micrositios/inicio.do" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: bibliotecavirtual.defensa.gob.es">[Biblioteca Virtual Defensa]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">bibliotecavirtual.defensa.gob.es</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on gob.es.</span></span></span>

The Agoncillo file also shows the limits of the public record. The Ministry explains that these files vary: some include summaries, witness interviews, incident reports, weather information, conclusions and classification proposals, while others are much shorter. Agoncillo is one of the short files, not a thick technical dossier. The catalogue lists only three pages, and contemporary summaries emphasise witness description rather than a chain of radar, meteorological, astronomical or air-traffic checks.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://bibliotecavirtual.defensa.gob.es/BVMDefensa/exp_ovni/es/micrositios/inicio.do" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: bibliotecavirtual.defensa.gob.es">[Biblioteca Virtual Defensa]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">bibliotecavirtual.defensa.gob.es</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on gob.es.</span></span></span>

For La Rioja, the file’s uniqueness is as significant as its contents. In the Defence library’s place index, La Rioja appears with one entry, and Logroño also appears with one entry. That does not mean there were no other local reports, but it does mean Agoncillo is the one La Rioja case that clearly entered the declassified national Defence UFO file set.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://bibliotecavirtual.defensa.gob.es/BVMDefensa/exp_ovni/es/consulta/indice_campo.do?campo=idlugar" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: bibliotecavirtual.defensa.gob.es">[Biblioteca Virtual Defensa]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">bibliotecavirtual.defensa.gob.es</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on gob.es.</span></span></span>

## Why Agoncillo was a serious place to report a strange light

The setting gives the case much of its weight. Agoncillo was not simply an ordinary village sky. The airfield had a long aviation history: Aena’s history of Logroño-Agoncillo Airport traces the site’s development from a military aerodrome, including its opening to official and private air traffic in the late 1920s and its later classification as a military base open to civil traffic.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.aena.es/en/logrono-agoncillo/conocenos/history.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: aena.es">[AENA]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">aena.es</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on aena.es.</span></span></span>

This aviation context cuts both ways. On one hand, military personnel at an aerodrome may be better placed than many civilians to notice aircraft-like behaviour, direction, altitude and sound. A report from a guarded airfield naturally carried more administrative weight than a casual street sighting. On the other hand, an aerodrome is precisely the kind of place where aircraft, navigation lights, military activity, training movements, approach lights, distant traffic or unusual viewing angles can complicate a night-time observation.

The site’s later history also helps modern readers understand why the case remains tied to La Rioja’s aviation geography. Aena records that the facilities were transferred to the Spanish Army in 1994, with the Army Airmobile Forces established there, and the modern civil airport was developed later. The 1978 case therefore belongs to a different operational era from today’s passenger airport, but it remains rooted in the same Agoncillo-Logroño aviation corridor.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.aena.es/es/logrono-agoncillo/conocenos/historia.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: aena.es">[AENA]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">aena.es</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on aena.es.</span></span></span>

That is why the case should not be stripped of its local setting. It is not merely “a UFO in La Rioja”. It is a reported anomalous aerial observation from a military aerodrome in a province with otherwise sparse official Defence UFO documentation. The location makes the report more interesting, while also broadening the range of ordinary [aviation explanations]({{ 'explanations-e27163/' | relative_url }}) that would need to be checked before calling it truly unexplained.

## What makes the evidence stronger than a local rumour

Agoncillo stands above most La Rioja UFO anecdotes because it has three features that many local sightings lack: named institutional custody, multiple witnesses, and a documented declassification trail. The record is held through the Biblioteca Virtual de Defensa, attributed to the Operational Air Command and the Air Force intelligence structure, and tied to a specific file number. Regional coverage in 2016 did not present the case as newly discovered folklore; it reported it in the context of the Ministry of Defence making its UFO files available online.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://bibliotecavirtual.defensa.gob.es/BVMDefensa/exp_ovni/en/consulta/registro.do?id=38342" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: bibliotecavirtual.defensa.gob.es">[Biblioteca Virtual Defensa]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">bibliotecavirtual.defensa.gob.es</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on gob.es.</span></span></span>

The multiple-witness element is also meaningful, though not decisive. The soldier’s initial observation was followed by the arrival of an ensign and a corporal, and all three reportedly saw the object. Their shared points — slow movement, silence, approximate height, east-west direction and white lights — give the report a stable core. Their disagreements over shape and light layout, however, remind us that multiple witnesses do not automatically produce precision. They can confirm that something was seen while still leaving the nature of that something uncertain.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.eldiario.es/la-rioja/ovnis-en-la-rioja-defensa-saca-a-la-luz-un-posible-caso-en-agoncillo_1_11094435.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: eldiario.es">[elDiario.es]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">eldiario.es</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ovnis en la rioja defensa saca a la luz un posible caso en agoncillo 1 11094435</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">OVNIS en La Rioja: Defensa saca a la luz un posible caso en Agoncillo...</span></span></span>

The declassification decision is sometimes misunderstood. Defence’s decision to declassify the file in 1995 does not mean the case was solved, nor does it mean it was secretly confirmed as extraordinary. The regional summary says Defence did not see aspects that justified keeping the material classified. In plain terms, the file was judged safe to release; that is different from a scientific conclusion about the object.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.eldiario.es/la-rioja/ovnis-en-la-rioja-defensa-saca-a-la-luz-un-posible-caso-en-agoncillo_1_11094435.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: eldiario.es">[elDiario.es]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">eldiario.es</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ovnis en la rioja defensa saca a la luz un posible caso en agoncillo 1 11094435</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">OVNIS en La Rioja: Defensa saca a la luz un posible caso en Agoncillo...</span></span></span>

## What still weakens the case

The main weakness is the thinness of the surviving public file. A three-page official record can preserve an incident, but it cannot do the work of a full modern investigation unless it contains detailed checks. The Ministry’s general description of the Defence collection notes that some files include weather reports, witness interviews and other supporting material, but also that each file is different and some are very short. Agoncillo’s catalogue entry lists only three pages, so the public record appears limited compared with more substantial Spanish cases.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://bibliotecavirtual.defensa.gob.es/BVMDefensa/exp_ovni/es/micrositios/inicio.do" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: bibliotecavirtual.defensa.gob.es">[Biblioteca Virtual Defensa]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">bibliotecavirtual.defensa.gob.es</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on gob.es.</span></span></span>

The second weakness is observational uncertainty. Estimated height, low speed and silence are useful descriptions, but without measured distance they can be misleading. A light that appears low may be higher and farther away; an object that appears slow may be moving across the viewer’s line of sight in a way that hides its true speed; silence may simply mean distance, wind, engine orientation or background conditions. None of those possibilities solves the case by itself, but they show why a short night-time sighting should not be overinterpreted.

The third weakness is the shape problem. A diamond or triangle seen at night may reflect the geometry of lights rather than the outline of a craft. The witnesses disagreed on whether the form was diamond-shaped or triangular, and reports differ over the exact number and placement of lights. This is exactly the kind of detail that often becomes sharper in retellings than it was in the original evidence.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.eldiario.es/la-rioja/ovnis-en-la-rioja-defensa-saca-a-la-luz-un-posible-caso-en-agoncillo_1_11094435.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: eldiario.es">[elDiario.es]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">eldiario.es</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ovnis en la rioja defensa saca a la luz un posible caso en agoncillo 1 11094435</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">OVNIS en La Rioja: Defensa saca a la luz un posible caso en Agoncillo...</span></span></span>

There is also no strong public indication that later reporting added decisive new evidence. The 2016 press coverage helped bring the file to a wider audience, but it largely summarised the Defence material rather than adding new witness interviews, photographs, radar confirmation or a solved explanation. Later database and archive references preserve the case as a documented entry, but they do not substantially change its evidential status.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://bluebookfiles.org/doc/8456" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: bluebookfiles.org">[Project Blue Book Archive]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">bluebookfiles.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Project Blue Book Archive Spanish UFOFiles</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Project Blue Book Archive Spanish UFOFiles</span></span></span>

## Why “unidentified” is the right conclusion

Agoncillo remains worth discussing because it resists two opposite mistakes. The first mistake is to dismiss it as mere folklore. It was recorded in an official Air Force file, entered the Defence UFO archive, and involved military witnesses at an aerodrome. For La Rioja, that makes it the province’s most documentable UFO case.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://bibliotecavirtual.defensa.gob.es/BVMDefensa/exp_ovni/en/consulta/registro.do?id=38342" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: bibliotecavirtual.defensa.gob.es">[Biblioteca Virtual Defensa]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">bibliotecavirtual.defensa.gob.es</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on gob.es.</span></span></span>

The second mistake is to treat the official file as proof of an extraordinary object. The record does not supply the kind of evidence that would support that claim. There is no public photograph, no radar plot cited in the summaries, no recovered material, no independent technical reconstruction and no clear elimination of ordinary explanations. What the file shows is that trained or semi-trained observers saw something they could not identify during a brief night watch, and that the report entered official channels.

A fair classification would be: unresolved in the public record, stronger than a casual anecdote, weaker than a multi-sensor aviation case. That may sound less exciting than the usual UFO framing, but it is more useful. It lets the case remain interesting without forcing it into either debunked triviality or extraterrestrial certainty.

This is also why Agoncillo works well as the central official case for La Rioja. It shows how a province can have a real place in Spain’s UFO archive even when the evidence is sparse. The value is not that the file proves a spectacular answer. The value is that it preserves a small, specific, unresolved report and gives readers a concrete example of how official UFO history often looks: brief, ambiguous, disciplined enough to record, but not strong enough to close.

## How it fits into La Rioja’s UFO history

Within La Rioja, Agoncillo is the anchor case because it is official, dated and traceable. Other local reports and flap stories may be more colourful, but they usually depend more heavily on local press, later recollection or paranormal-interest retellings. Agoncillo has the advantage of a Defence catalogue entry, a file number, a military setting and a declassification note.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://bibliotecavirtual.defensa.gob.es/BVMDefensa/exp_ovni/en/consulta/registro.do?id=38342" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: bibliotecavirtual.defensa.gob.es">[Biblioteca Virtual Defensa]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">bibliotecavirtual.defensa.gob.es</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on gob.es.</span></span></span>

That does not make it the whole story of UFOs in the province. Regional reporting has pointed to other La Rioja claims, including mid-1970s sightings around Logroño, Arrúbal and Arnedo, as well as later local reports. But those belong to a broader page on La Rioja’s sighting clusters and local UFO folklore. The Agoncillo page should stay narrower: one aerodrome, one night, three witnesses, one official file, and one unresolved evidential question.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.eldiario.es/la-rioja/ovnis-en-la-rioja-defensa-saca-a-la-luz-un-posible-caso-en-agoncillo_1_11094435.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: eldiario.es">[elDiario.es]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">eldiario.es</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ovnis en la rioja defensa saca a la luz un posible caso en agoncillo 1 11094435</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">OVNIS en La Rioja: Defensa saca a la luz un posible caso en Agoncillo...</span></span></span>

The case also helps set standards for reading the weaker material. If even La Rioja’s best official case cannot be treated as proof of an extraordinary craft, then less documented cases should be handled with still more caution. Conversely, if a case has official custody and military witnesses, it should not be waved away simply because the object remains unidentified.

Agoncillo therefore matters less as a mystery to be solved from a distance than as a benchmark. It marks the strongest public Defence record for La Rioja, shows how little an official UFO file may actually contain, and keeps the province’s UFO history grounded in evidence rather than in exaggeration.

    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var picker = section.querySelector('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
    if (menu) menu.hidden = true;
    if (trigger) trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', 'false');
    if (picker) picker.classList.remove('fr-ebay-market-picker--open');
  }
  function availableMarkets(section) {
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
