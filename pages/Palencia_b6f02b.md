## Introduction

The case matters because it shows how UFO stories entered official Spanish channels: residents saw an unusual orange light, the air base at Villanubla received repeated calls, a telegram went up the chain of command, and the event became file 681207 in the Air Force’s UFO archive. It remains interesting, but not strong evidence of anything exotic. Later sceptical cataloguing classed the likely explanation as the planet Venus, which was a recurring source of misidentified sightings during the late-[1968 wave]({{ '1968-wave/' | relative_url }}).<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://inexplicata.blogspot.com/2017/05/spain-government-dossier-on-villalon-de.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: inexplicata.blogspot.com">[inexplicata.blogspot.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">inexplicata.blogspot.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">spain government dossier on villalon de</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">spain government dossier on villalon de</span></span></span>

## What happened over Tierra de Campos in December 1968?

The core report is simple. Around 19:15 on Saturday 7 December 1968, witnesses in the Tierra de Campos area reported seeing an orange light in the sky. Later summaries describe the object as rising or moving upwards and disappearing after roughly three minutes. The first caller is said to have been a lorry driver from Mazariegos, followed by other lorry drivers, residents around Villalón, and people from Palencia.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://inexplicata.blogspot.com/2017/05/spain-government-dossier-on-villalon-de.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: inexplicata.blogspot.com">[inexplicata.blogspot.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">inexplicata.blogspot.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">spain government dossier on villalon de</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">spain government dossier on villalon de</span></span></span>

The calls went to the Villanubla air base, near Valladolid. According to later reporting based on the official dossier, the persistence of the calls led the base commandant to send a telegram to the Air Ministry at about 00:50 that night, noting the phenomenon and adding that there was no other news elsewhere in the region. The result was an official intelligence file: expediente 681207, “sighting of strange phenomena in Villalón de Campos (Palencia): 7 December 1968”.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://inexplicata.blogspot.com/2017/05/spain-government-dossier-on-villalon-de.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: inexplicata.blogspot.com">[inexplicata.blogspot.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">inexplicata.blogspot.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">spain government dossier on villalon de</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">spain government dossier on villalon de</span></span></span>

For Palencia readers, the geography needs care. The official Defence catalogue records both “Villalón de Campos” and “Palencia province” in the subject fields, while regional tourism sources identify Villalón de Campos as part of Valladolid. Mazariegos, one of the witness locations named in later accounts, is a Palencia municipality, and the case was also linked to calls from Palencia itself. That makes it best understood as a Tierra de Campos border-area incident with a real Palencia component, rather than a cleanly Palencia-only case. ## Why this modest case became Palencia’s key UFO file

The Villalón-Mazariegos-Palencia sighting is important less because of what was seen than because of how it was handled. Spain’s Ministry of Defence explains that the Air Force UFO files were declassified from 1991 onwards and later digitised through the Defence Virtual Library. The collection contains 80 files and about 1,900 pages covering reports from 1962 to 1995 in which Air Force personnel or equipment were involved in some way.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://bibliotecavirtual.defensa.gob.es/BVMDefensa/exp_ovni/es/micrositios/inicio.do" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: bibliotecavirtual.defensa.gob.es">[Biblioteca Virtual Defensa]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">bibliotecavirtual.defensa.gob.es</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on gob.es.</span></span></span>

File 681207 is part of that official corpus. The Defence catalogue gives it four pages, names the Air Force operational command and intelligence section, and notes that it was declassified under a 21 April 1993 order. The online record also lists its subject as UFO observations and encounters, with Villalón de Campos, Valladolid province and Palencia province all appearing in the metadata.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://bibliotecavirtual.defensa.gob.es/BVMDefensa/exp_ovni/es/consulta/busqueda_referencia.do?campo=idtitulo&amp;idValor=395922" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: bibliotecavirtual.defensa.gob.es">[Biblioteca Virtual Defensa]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">bibliotecavirtual.defensa.gob.es</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on gob.es.</span></span></span>

## The 1968 wave changed how people reported lights in the sky

The Palencia-area sighting happened during a noisy period for Spanish UFO reporting. Contemporary and later accounts describe 1968 as a year of heavy UFO interest, with the government investigating numerous cases and the Air Ministry issuing public instructions asking people who believed they had seen unidentified objects to notify the nearest Air Force or local authority. That public appeal appeared just one day before the Tierra de Campos calls.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://inexplicata.blogspot.com/2017/05/spain-government-dossier-on-villalon-de.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: inexplicata.blogspot.com">[inexplicata.blogspot.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">inexplicata.blogspot.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">spain government dossier on villalon de</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">spain government dossier on villalon de</span></span></span>

This context matters because it can create a feedback loop. Once newspapers, official notices and neighbours are discussing UFOs, ordinary ambiguous lights become more reportable. A lorry driver who might otherwise have ignored a bright object near the horizon may decide to telephone the air base. Several independent calls can still describe the same mundane object, especially when people are primed to look up and interpret the sky through the language of UFOs.

The same wave also produced nearby Castile and León cases, including Puente Almuhey in León, where witnesses reported red, slow-moving lights over several dates in late November and early December 1968. Regional coverage of the declassified files lists five Castile and León cases in the Defence archive: two near Burgos, the Villalón file, Villanubla, and Puente Almuhey.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.diariodevalladolid.es/valladolid/161025/129413/ovnis-pasaron-valladolid.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: diariodevalladolid.es">[Diario de Valladolid]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">diariodevalladolid.es</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ovnis pasaron valladolid</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">ovnis pasaron valladolid</span></span></span>

## The strongest explanation is less strange than the first impression

The best sceptical reading is that the 7 December [1968 sighting]({{ '1968-sighting-08de36/' | relative_url }}) was probably Venus, not an aircraft, secret weapon or alien craft. A later breakdown of the Spanish Air Force UFO archive lists the Villalón de Campos case at 19:15, classifies it as a nocturnal light reported from the ground, and gives its assessment as “probable planet Venus”. The same catalogue also identifies several late-1968 Spanish cases as Venus or possible Venus, including Constantina, Puente Almuhey, Madrid and El Garrobo.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://elojocritico.info/los-archivos-ovni-del-ejercito-del-aire-desglosados-quien-que-como/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: elojocritico.info">[elojocritico.info]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">elojocritico.info</span><span class="citation-popover-title">los archivos ovni del ejercito del aire desglosados quien que como</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">los archivos ovni del ejercito del aire desglosados quien que como</span></span></span>

That explanation fits several features of the Palencia-area account. Venus is extremely bright, often visible around dusk, and can look startling when seen low in a clear evening sky. It does not need to move in order to seem unusual: changing viewing angles from a moving vehicle, passing cloud, atmospheric haze, and the short duration of witness attention can all create the impression of motion or disappearance. The description of an orange light, seen around early evening, with no recorded shape, size or physical trace, is not enough to rule out a bright astronomical object.

There are still reasons not to call the case “solved” with absolute certainty. The witness reports described an ascending movement, while Venus itself does not shoot upwards over three minutes. The available public summaries are also brief, and the underlying file is small. But the evidential balance is clear: multiple phone calls prove that people saw something they found unusual; they do not prove that the object itself was unusual.

## What the evidence can and cannot support

The Palencia case has three relatively firm supports. First, there is an official Defence Library record for a 7 December 1968 file, with its title, date, location, Air Force authorship and declassification note. Second, regional reporting and a specialist translation describe multiple calls to Villanubla air base from Villalón, Mazariegos and Palencia. Third, later sceptical cataloguing gives a specific conventional explanation: probable Venus.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://bibliotecavirtual.defensa.gob.es/BVMDefensa/exp_ovni/es/consulta/busqueda_referencia.do?campo=idtitulo&amp;idValor=395922" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: bibliotecavirtual.defensa.gob.es">[Biblioteca Virtual Defensa+2inexplicata.blogspot.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">bibliotecavirtual.defensa.gob.es</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on gob.es.</span></span></span>

What is missing is just as important. There is no widely cited radar confirmation, no photograph, no physical trace, no pilot encounter, and no detailed technical reconstruction showing that the reported light could not have been Venus, a meteor, aircraft light or other ordinary source. The official archive entry confirms a report and an investigation; it does not elevate the observation into a landmark unexplained event.

For a public-facing history of UFOs in Palencia, the honest classification is therefore: **documented but weakly evidential; historically interesting; probably explained**. It belongs in the province’s UFO story because it shows how Palencia residents and nearby Tierra de Campos witnesses interacted with Spain’s official UFO-reporting machinery during the 1968 wave. It should not be presented as a strong unresolved encounter.

## How Palencia fits the wider Castile and León UFO map

Palencia does not appear to have the same density of famous UFO lore as some Spanish hotspots. Its role is more regional and archival: a borderland sighting in Tierra de Campos, a connection to Villanubla air base, and overlap with neighbouring Valladolid and León cases. That makes the province useful for understanding how UFO reports spread across rural Castile and León rather than for building a catalogue of spectacular close encounters.

The pattern is familiar. Witnesses see a light; local calls reach an aviation or military authority; a short file is opened; later declassification gives the case a second life in newspapers and online archives. In Palencia’s case, later reporting did not strengthen the original claim. It clarified the witness chain and official handling, but it also made the conventional explanation more visible.

The most responsible way to read the Palencia material is not as a hidden secret, but as a small case study in perception, media climate and military record-keeping. It captures a moment when ordinary people in Tierra de Campos were watching the evening sky with unusual attention, and when the Spanish Air Force was sufficiently concerned by public interest to tell citizens where to report what they believed were UFOs.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://inexplicata.blogspot.com/2017/05/spain-government-dossier-on-villalon-de.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: inexplicata.blogspot.com">[inexplicata.blogspot.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">inexplicata.blogspot.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">spain government dossier on villalon de</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">spain government dossier on villalon de</span></span></span>

    var template = String(cfg.url_template || 'https://www.ebay.com/sch/i.html?_nkw={query}');
    var url = template.replace('{query}', encodeQuery(query || '')).replace('{query_plain}', String(query || ''));
    var joiner = url.indexOf('?') >= 0 ? '&' : '?';
    var params = [];
    if (cfg.mkrid) params.push('mkrid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.mkrid));
    if (cfg.campaign_id) params.push('campid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.campaign_id));
    params.push('mkevt=1');
    params.push('mkcid=1');
    params.push('toolid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.tool_id || '10001'));
    if (reference) params.push('customid=' + encodeURIComponent(reference).slice(0, 256));
    return url + joiner + params.join('&');
  }
  function domainLabel(marketId) {
    var cfg = markets[marketId] || markets[defaultMarket] || {};
    return cfg.domain_label || 'eBay';
  }
  function flagClass(marketId) {
    return 'fr-ebay-market-flag fr-ebay-market-flag--' + String(marketId || '').toLowerCase().replace(/_/g, '-').replace(/[^a-z0-9-]+/g, '');
  }
  function closeMenu(section) {
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var picker = section.querySelector('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
    if (menu) menu.hidden = true;
    if (trigger) trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', 'false');
    if (picker) picker.classList.remove('fr-ebay-market-picker--open');
  }
  function availableMarkets(section) {
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
